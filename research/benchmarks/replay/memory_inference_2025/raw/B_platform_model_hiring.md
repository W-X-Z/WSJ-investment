## Hardware-facing terms that recur across sub-areas (5 lines)

1. **KV cache offload tiers (GPU HBM → CPU DRAM/LPDDR → local NVMe SSD → networked/object storage)** appear in every platform (Dynamo, llm-d, LMCache, SGLang HiCache with a 2x host-to-GPU pool, TRT-LLM 1.0 GB200 host memory, NIXL GDS/POSIX plugins). On the API side the same pattern shows up as **cache discounts of 75–90% and longer TTLs** (GPT-4.1, Gemini implicit caching, Anthropic 1 h, xAI). Retained KV became something providers price and sell between April and September 2025.
2. **Context windows of 1M–10M tokens** (GPT-4.1, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Llama 4 Scout, Grok 4 Fast, Qwen3-Coder) come with **tiered long-context pricing** and stated KV sizes per user (about 40 GB at 128K on Llama-3-70B). Low precision (FP8 KV, NVFP4/MXFP4 weights) is used to **fit more context and batch into HBM**, not to shrink total memory. SGLang lost about 10% throughput when its KV cache was not full.
3. **Long-horizon agents and coding agents** grew from 7 h (Opus 4, May) to more than 30 h (Sonnet 4.5, Sep). Coding-agent sessions run more than 25K tokens over about 8 turns, cache hits exceed 90%, and Claude Code usage grew more than 10x in three months. The binding resource is KV/state persisted across turns and hours, i.e. capacity tiers more than bandwidth.
4. **Disaggregated prefill/decode and wide expert parallelism** move KV and tokens over **RDMA (InfiniBand/RoCE), multi-node NVLink (MNNVL/NVL72) and NIXL/Mooncake/UCX**. Recurring details: one RDMA NIC per GPU, NVLink replacing RDMA inside GB200 racks, and 800 Gbps non-blocking fat-trees at Fairwater. Counter-signals are hybrid-linear and sparse attention (MiniMax-M1, Qwen3-Next, DeepSeek DSA), which cut KV bytes or FLOPs per token, though DSA keeps a dense 656 B/token KV.
5. **Volume and power context:** monthly tokens grew 50x YoY at Google (480T→980T in about two months) and 5–7x at Microsoft. GW-scale, liquid-cooled builds followed: Microsoft added more than 2 GW and made every region liquid-cooling capable; Fairwater is more than 90% closed-loop liquid; Stargate plans about 7 GW and targets 10 GW; OpenAI and NVIDIA signed for 10 GW. Inference serving pairs every added GPU with more attached memory and storage.
