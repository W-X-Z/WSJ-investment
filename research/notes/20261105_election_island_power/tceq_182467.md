## 4. Legal force of Abbott's directive; litigation; Hinojosa; Patrick/Paxton

### 4a. Instrument
- It is a **letter**, not an executive order. Verbatim (gov.texas.gov PDF, Sept 21, 2026, to Executive Director Kelly Keel): "With this letter I am directing the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) to halt all permits sought by data centers." "...no other state agency shall move forward with regulatory approvals related to data centers until this information is acquired. Therefore, I direct the TCEQ to pause the issuance of all permits related to data center projects until ERCOT completes its review." "Data center projects must cover all electrical infrastructure costs, result in lower residential electrical bills, and complete the ERCOT and TWDB audits. Further, data centers must not use water needed by local communities, must report electricity and water usage, and must abide by setback requirements..." "Finally, I will work with the Legislature to eliminate any financial incentives for data centers. TCEQ will play a vital role once these laws are enacted. I am also engaging your agency now, before these additional safeguards are codified..." "As ERCOT and TWDB conduct their audits, TCEQ must similarly ensure that its permitting decisions align with my directives." "TCEQ shall provide an update to my office on its compliance with my directive by Monday, October 19, 2026." Signed "Greg Abbott Governor". URL: https://gov.texas.gov/uploads/files/press/TCEQ_Data_Center.pdf
- Press release same day: "Until they do, TCEQ will issue no permits sought by data center projects." URL: https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-directs-tceq-to-halt-data-center-permits
- No exemption for off-grid/BTM: DLA Piper (Sept 25, 2026): the directive "does not expressly exclude data centers that do not require a grid connection." B&D: no exception for "projects using on-site generation." Bracewell: pause applies to "fully 'islanded' facilities relying on behind-the-meter generation."

### 4b. Authority / legal force commentary
- B&D (Sept 29, 2026): the directive "does not amend statutes and rules governing individual TCEQ programs"; cites HSC §382.0518(b) ("shall grant within a reasonable time").
- San Antonio Current (Sept 29–30, 2026): Adrian Shelley (Public Citizen): "TCEQ has said before they don't have the authority to halt all permits for a given industry." Luke Metzger (Environment Texas): the most impactful proposals "really do require legislative approval." TCEQ Air Permits Division director Samuel Short: "At the direction of Governor Greg Abbott, the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) has paused the issuance..." Article reports no lawsuit filed or threatened; Metzger suggests companies may comply rather than sue. URL: https://www.sacurrent.com/news/abbott-orders-pause-of-air-permits-for-data-centers-experts-question-whether-he-has-the-authority/
- KERA (Aug 5, 2026, on the Aug 3 ERCOT directive): Public Citizen: "Absent a special session, the governor's powers are limited." Ag Commissioner Sid Miller (R): "Without legislative action, this directive is all hat and no cattle." ERCOT and PUCT asked lawmakers to expand their authority.
- Texas Tribune (Sept 21, 2026): July 17 PUCT Chair Thomas Gleeson letter "calling on the Legislature for more regulatory authority."
- **Lawsuits**: none found challenging the Sept 21 TCEQ directive or the Aug 3 ERCOT directive as of Nov 5, 2026 (searches: "ultra vires", "sued", "legal challenge"). Only related suits found: a developer's May 2026 suit against a **Hill County** local moratorium (KWTX, May 28, 2026) and environmental groups' suits over San Antonio data-center air permits — neither challenges the Governor.
- TCEQ compliance posture is voluntary/administrative: Oct 19 Schar letter (above); TCEQ commissioners are gubernatorial appointees (Paup, Gonzales, Miller per letterhead). **[INFERENCE: no court has ruled; the pause rests on agency acquiescence.]**

## 6. ERCOT audit and PUCT $50,000/MW rule

### 6a. ERCOT audit (primary: ERCOT PUCT presentation Aug 20, 2026; ERCOT Board item 14, Sept 14–15, 2026)
- Abbott Aug 3, 2026 directive: "a comprehensive verification and audit" of all data centers advancing through the interconnection process before allowing those loads to proceed.
- ERCOT actions: "August 3, 2026 – Issued a Market Notice pausing the Batch Zero study process and delaying final classification of all large loads"; "Paused approvals to energize data centers or virtual currency mining facilities (crypto facilities) that are 75 MW or greater."
- Two workstreams: (A) "Batch Zero Eligibility Verification — ERCOT will confirm that all large loads conditionally included in Batch Zero that have yet to energize satisfy the applicable eligibility criteria under PGRR 145"; (B) "Community Impact Review — ERCOT will request information from data center and crypto facilities that have requested 25 MW or more and have yet to energize."
- Timing: "December 10 File Batch Zero Eligibility Verification Report"; "December 17 Commission Open Meeting"; "ERCOT will file the Community Impact Review Report with the Commission by December 10, 2026."
- Scope figures: "204 projects (66.4 GW) met the qualifications to be conditionally included as base load; 158 projects (127.9 GW) ... studied load"; chart "omits excluded load (302.2 GW, 373 projects)". Studied load includes a "WLPUN (BYOG)" category.
- Batch Zero study deadline "April 9, 2027" — "ERCOT is still determining the impact to the Batch Zero Study timeline".
- URLs: https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/08/20/Batch-Zero-Verification-and-Audit-PUCT-Presentation.pdf ; https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/11/14-Batch-Zero-Update.pdf
- **BTM/islanded coverage**: the audit is defined around loads "advancing through the interconnection process" / "that seek to interconnect to the ERCOT system" and the RFI goes to "Interconnecting TSPs and DSPs". A fully islanded plant with no interconnection request would not be in Batch Zero. **[INFERENCE: whether Vantage Frontier/VoltaGrid has any ERCOT interconnection request (e.g., for backup/supplemental grid supply) is unknown; if none, ERCOT's audit cannot "clear" it, yet Abbott's TCEQ pause is keyed to ERCOT completing its review.]** Texas Tribune (Sept 21, 2026) noted some data centers that build "behind the meter" could avoid the ERCOT audit delay. B&D: RFI responses due "October 12, 2026, at 5 pm Central Time"; report "No later than December 10, 2026."

### 6b. PUCT $50,000/MW (White & Case, Sept 2026; PUCT Project No. 58481, order Sept 18, 2026; 16 TAC §25.194; PURA §37.0561 via SB 6, 2025)
- Applies to "all large loads equal to or exceeding 75 megawatts (MW) at a single site" in ERCOT.
- It is **financial security, not a fee**: the final rule "shifts the $50,000 per MW to become financial security posted" (proposal had been a non-refundable fee). Intermediate Agreement: $50,000/MW of requested peak demand before the interconnection study, plus a $100,000 study fee. SLLIA: security equal to the greater of $50,000/MW of contracted peak demand or allocated transmission upgrade costs. Release: 20% at initial energization, remaining 80% in 20% increments at minimum-billing-demand milestones. Withdrawal after capacity allocation forfeits 20%.
- PUCT "rejected requests to exempt co-located generation" and calculates security on "gross contracted peak demand, not net grid imports." Not limited to data centers.
- URL: https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/puct-sets-financial-commitments-data-center-interconnection-governor-pauses-permit
- **[INFERENCE]** The rule attaches to interconnection; a purely islanded load posts nothing under it.
